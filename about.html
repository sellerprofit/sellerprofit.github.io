About this site

This site is a free set of tools for people who sell online. It started with one question that comes up all the time: after Etsy takes its fees, how much do I actually keep?

The calculator shows your profit on a sale, your break-even price, and the price you need to charge to hit a profit or margin goal. You can change every fee rate, so it also works for shops outside the US and UK.

Be aware

The results are estimates. Etsy can change its fees at any time, and your own situation (taxes, currency conversion, subscriptions) can change what you pay. Always check Etsy's official fee page before making pricing decisions. This site is not affiliated with Etsy.
