How Etsy fees add up

On a typical US sale, Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing of 3% plus $0.25. The transaction fee and processing fee are both charged on the item price plus the shipping you charge, so shipping is not a way around them.

What Offsite Ads cost

If a buyer clicks one of Etsy's ads on another site and buys from your shop within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% of the order. Shops above $10,000 in yearly sales pay 12%. In the US the fee is capped at $100 per order, and smaller shops can usually opt out in their settings.

UK sellers: VAT and the regulatory fee

UK sellers pay a higher processing rate than US sellers, plus a small regulatory operating fee on each sale, and VAT is added on top of Etsy's fees. If you are VAT-registered you may be able to reclaim that VAT, so set the VAT rate to 0 under "Edit fee rates" to see your figures without it.

Pricing for profit, not guesses

Your break-even price is the lowest item price where you neither make nor lose money after Etsy's fees, your item cost, and your real shipping cost. Anything above it is profit. The price table shows how fast profit grows when you raise your price, because fees take a percentage but your costs stay fixed. A small increase often adds more to your profit than it appears.

Why cheap items lose the most

The fixed fees (listing plus the processing fixed amount) take a much bigger share of a $5 item than a $50 one. If your margins feel thin on low-priced items, test a higher price or a shipping charge here before you list.

Common questions

How much does Etsy take from a $100 sale?

For a US seller with free shipping, Etsy takes $9.95: a $0.20 listing fee, a $6.50 transaction fee (6.5%), and $3.25 in payment processing (3% plus $0.25). If the sale comes from an Offsite Ad at 15%, add $15.00 for $24.95 in total.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee and the payment processing fee are charged on the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer.

Can I opt out of Offsite Ads fees?

Shops with under $10,000 in sales over the past 365 days can opt out in their shop settings. Once a shop passes that amount, Offsite Ads are mandatory and the fee drops from 15% to 12%.
