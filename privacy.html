What this site does

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to our server and are not stored. If you press "Copy link to these numbers", the numbers are put into a link that you choose where to share.

We do not ask you to create an account or give personal details. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own cookies. If we add an analytics tool in future, we will update this page.

Children
